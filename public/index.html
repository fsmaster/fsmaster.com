HAR Fixer — Repair HARs for Google HAR Analyzer

A support-engineering tool that fixes the customer HAR files Google HAR Analyzer rejects: sanitized bodies still flagged as base64, malformed JSON, truncated exports and missing fields. Built by reverse-engineering the analyzer's loader, which the tool replays to show which request crashes it and why. LeakCheck — What Your Browser Tells Every Website

fsmaster.com/leakcheck

A browser-fingerprint and leak inspector that shows ~200 signals anti-fraud tools like SEON, Castle and FingerprintJS use to recognize a device: IP and WebRTC leaks, TLS cipher list, Client Hints, unmasked GPU, canvas and audio hashes, fonts, time zone and more. It flags the inconsistencies risk engines score, and compares two browsers to show whether they can be linked. No storage, no cookies; the server only echoes the request back.
